ABOUT GREENSTEELS • DINDIGUL

Reliable Steel Centring & Fabrication Works Since Day One

Based in Bismi Nagar, Batlagundu Road, Dindigul, GreenSteels is an authentic Indian steel manufacturing and structural fabrication unit. We specialize in robust, high-repetition centring plates, heavy shuttering door clamps, and custom drilled steel base hardware.

Building contractors and formwork rental agencies across Tamil Nadu rely on our equipment because we never compromise on mild steel sheet gauge or welding integrity. Every plate is reinforced with heavy angle framing, tested for flat surface uniformity, and finished for maximum operational lifecycles.
